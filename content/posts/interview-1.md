> We are working on a new project in ACME corp. ACME builds a data/business analytics platform. Their customers connect their data warehouses and bring in their data. On this data they build analytics and dashboards. We want to improve the capabilities of our analytics platform and provide a feature of building predictive models. This feature will be provided via an agent and the interface by which the user is interacting is chat. For example, user can come on to the chat window and ask for building a predictive model.
